Europe's gigafactory build-out: where the capacity really is

By Velstrand Research analysts · December 2025

Announced gigafactory projects in Europe far exceed the capacity that is actually operational today. A handful of sites account for most of the real output.

The 2030 pipeline remains ambitious, but several projects have slipped and investment per GWh varies widely from one site to another.

Our full report lists operational capacity by site in 2025, the planned 2030 capacity and the associated investment.
