About Velstrand Research

Velstrand Research is an independent research firm covering the European battery value chain, from lithium supply to cell manufacturing and gigafactory build-out.

Our analysts combine plant-level shipment data, customs records and interviews with industry contacts to publish proprietary market data used by investors, automakers and utilities.

Velstrand Research is a fictional company created for a hackathon demo. Every company name and every figure on this site is invented.
